My Journey in Software Development

SENA and Self-Learning Online

My path in technology has been a mix of formal studies and personal effort. Today I am a software development student at SENA, in the program Tecnología en Análisis y Desarrollo de Software. At the same time, I also learn on my own through online resources. This combination has helped me grow step by step.

Studying at SENA: building strong foundations

At SENA, I am learning important topics that give structure to my professional goal. The program helps me understand how software is planned, developed, tested, and improved. I am not only learning to write code, but also to think like a developer: solving problems, working with logic, and building solutions that can help people. Studying in a program like this also gives me discipline. I have deadlines, projects, and a learning process that prepares me for real work situations.

Self-learning online: practicing and going deeper

Outside of SENA, I spend time learning online. This helps me practice more and explore topics that interest me. Some days I focus on programming, other days I focus on tools, or new concepts that I want to understand better. Online learning is not always easy, because you must be consistent. But it is powerful, because you can learn at your own pace and choose what you need.

Learning English and soft skills

Another important part of my journey is learning English. English opens many doors in technology because a lot of documentation, tutorials, and communities are in English. Even if my level is not perfect, I keep practicing because it helps me understand more and feel more confident. I also work on soft skills, like: Communication, Teamwork, Time management, Responsibility and discipline These skills matter a lot, because software development is not only about code. It is also about working with people and building solutions together.

Final thoughts

I am still learning, and I know the journey takes time. But I feel proud because I am building my future with effort, consistency, and curiosity. SENA gives me the foundation, and self-learning helps me grow faster and stay motivated.

Are you studying software development too, or learning online? What is the biggest challenge for you right now?
